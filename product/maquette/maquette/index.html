    SharedHousehold — Product maquette¶

    See the product before it exists: interactive mockup at docs/product/maquette/mockups/app.html (open it directly in any browser, nothing to install) and the wireframes below. Designer agent: designer. Issue reference: none yet — this is the first design deliverable.

    Purpose¶

    Show the owner and the implementers what the SharedHousehold app should look and feel like, mapped to the domain in AGENTS.md:

    • who paid
    • who participated
    • who owes
    • how the amount was allocated
    • how a debt was settled

    The mockup is data-only (no backend). Every number is deterministic and explicit, because the app is a household ledger, not a splitter.

    Design principles¶

    1. Calm & trustworthy — money feels safe: soft green accent, generous whitespace, tabular numerals, no alarmist UI.
    2. Explicit allocation — every expense shows who paid, who participated, and the split rule, never hidden inside a "total".
    3. Mobile-first — the living room couch is the primary surface; desktop only adapts the same screens.
    4. Zero-friction settle-up — balances are always visible and once-a-screen settlement suggested, because that's the reason people come back.
    5. Offline-first visuals — no external fonts, CDNs, or icons; the mockup opens from the repo and works forever.

    Design decisions (mockup)¶

    • Colors: base #f6f7f4 page, surface white, ink #171b17, accent emerald #0f8a5f, owe-red #c2543f used sparingly (balances only).
    • Currency: CHF placeholder; the ledger keeps one currency per household (no multi-currency in MVP).
    • Add expense split: MVP shows equal + custom only (shares/percentage are the planned next step, so the UI already reserves the "custom" affordance).
    • Settlement: proposed transfers reduce the number of payments (simplified debts); recording one is an explicit ledger event with a participant/link.

    In the mockup but not the MVP¶

    • Everything is client-side and fake-data; no persistence or auth flows.
    • The "Products / shared goods" area from the roadmap is NOT in this mockup — it will be designed when that roadmap phase becomes real work.

    How to use¶

    1. Open mockups/app.html in a browser.
    2. Click through: Dashboard → add expense → balances → settle up → members.
    3. Send feedback as a GitHub issue titled with the screen name; the autonomy pipeline picks it up, or a designer agent iterates the maquette.
